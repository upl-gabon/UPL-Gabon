Institution

The University

Since 2022, UPL has been running an Executive MBA in Libreville for executives and managers, with the academic support of the University of Douala.

Campus: Sablière, Libreville. President: Serge Patrick MINANG, engineer, MBA, DBA candidate.

UPL's vocation is to become, over time, a leading private university in Gabon and Central Africa.

— Serge Patrick MINANG, Founder & President
Open programme

Executive MBA

Admissions open

For working executives and managers. Evening classes (5–9 pm) in Sablière. Class size of about 20 students. Tuition: 4,000,000 FCFA per year, payable in instalments.
