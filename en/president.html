Dear visitors,

The Private University of Libreville was born of a simple conviction: Gabon and Central Africa need solid managers, trained with rigour and close to the field. Since 2022, our Executive MBA, run with the University of Douala, has been supporting executives and managers who keep working while they study.

Nearly eighty executives have already followed this path. Classes are held in the evening, from 5:00 pm to 9:00 pm, in Sablière: you learn without interrupting your career. The modules — business law, financial accounting, strategic management, quality management, marketing, case studies — address the real situations our students face in their organisations.

Our ambition is clear: to make UPL, over time, a leading private university in Gabon and Central Africa. We work towards it at every intake — by opening new programmes, upholding the quality of our teaching and staying true to our values: Excellence, Innovation, Leadership.

If you are an executive or a manager and wish to structure your management practice, come and meet us in Sablière or write to us: the Secretariat will provide the application file and will answer you.

Serge Patrick MINANG

Founder & President — Private University of Libreville
