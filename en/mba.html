The programme

The Executive MBA is designed for working professionals (public administration, public companies, the private sector) who want to strengthen their management and governance practice.

Run in Libreville, with academic support from the University of Douala (partnership agreement).

  • Audience: executives and managers
  • Evening classes — compatible with a professional activity
  • Location: Sablière, Libreville
  • Since 2022: nearly 80 executives trained

Organisation

Classes run in the evening, from 5:00 pm to 9:00 pm, in 4-hour sessions, in Sablière. Each module represents about 20 hours and concludes with an assessment.

The final timetable is handed out at the start of each module by the Secretariat.
