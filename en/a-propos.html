About UPL

Private higher-education institution · Libreville · Gabon.

The institution

The Private University of Libreville (UPL) is a private higher-education institution. Since 2022, it has been running an Executive MBA for executives and managers, with the academic support of the University of Douala.

President: Serge Patrick MINANG (engineer, MBA, DBA candidate). Campus: Sablière district, Libreville.

Vision

UPL's vocation is to become, over time, a leading private university in Gabon and Central Africa. After four years of the Executive MBA, the university is widening its offer: for the 2026-2027 academic year, UPL has opened pre-registration: bachelor's degrees, master's degrees, preparatory classes (CPGE), the Executive MBA and the DBA, delivered through five teaching poles. Full programme and tuition details are presented on the homepage.
